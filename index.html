04 — PRINCIPLES

How I keep AI honest

Three rules run through everything above. They are enforced in code, not requested in a prompt.

/ 01

Refuse to guess

A score floor and "no citation, no answer," so a weak match becomes "I don't have that" instead of a confident mistake.

/ 02

Prove it

Gold-set evaluation that scores correctness and citations separately — measured, not asserted, and never a single number you can game.

/ 03

Gate it

A regression gate on every prompt, model, or ingest change that blocks the deploy when quality drifts down.
